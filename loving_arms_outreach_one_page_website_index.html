Our Mission

To serve families in underserved communities across Atlanta, Greene, Rockdale, and Newton counties by providing healthy food, essential household items, and practical support — while connecting people to education, resources, and caring relationships that build stability and hope.

  • Weekly food assistance and mobile distributions
  • Seasonal drives (school supplies, coats, toys, holiday meals)
  • Nutrition and budget‑friendly cooking workshops
  • Senior outreach and wellness check‑ins

Our Vision

We envision communities where no neighbor goes without a meal, every child shows up to school prepared, and seniors receive the respect and support they deserve. By partnering with local leaders, faith communities, and civic organizations, we scale what works and build resilient neighborhoods — one act of care at a time.
